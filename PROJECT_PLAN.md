# Project Plan

## Project Title

NovoTech Sales Analytics

---

## Business Problem

NovoTech is a national consumer electronics retailer operating both physical stores and an online sales channel.

Although sales have continued to grow, the company's profit margins have been declining over recent months.

The Commercial Director has requested a complete sales analysis to identify the factors affecting profitability and support strategic decision-making.

---

## Project Goal

Design and build a complete Business Intelligence solution using MySQL and Power BI to answer key business questions related to sales performance, customer behavior and product profitability.

---

## Main Objectives

- Design a relational database from a transactional dataset.
- Import and organize data into MySQL.
- Perform SQL analysis to answer business questions.
- Build an executive dashboard in Power BI.
- Present actionable business insights and recommendations.
